# Differentiated HR Solution Design

## 1. A solution is a mechanism

Do not confuse an HR program category with a solution.

"Mentoring" is a category.
"AI" is a technology.
"Training" is an activity.
"Recognition" is a tool.

A solution explains how behavior and outcomes change.

Use:

Target
+ Trigger
+ Intervention
+ Mechanism
+ Workflow
+ Owner
+ Data
+ Incentive
+ Feedback loop
+ Measurement.

## 3. Create originality through recombination

Originality does not require science fiction.

Differentiate through one or more of:

### Targeting
Solve for a precise high-value population.

### Timing
Intervene at the moment risk or opportunity emerges.

### Decision architecture
Change who decides, with what information, using what rule.

### Workflow
Embed the intervention inside real work rather than as a separate HR program.

### Incentive
Change payoff, accountability, recognition, or resource allocation.

### Information
Expose hidden opportunities, skills, risks, or trade-offs.

### Matching
Improve matching between people and roles, projects, mentors, learning, shifts, or opportunities.

### Personalization
Adapt intervention intensity/content to context.

### Marketplace design
Create transparent internal supply-demand mechanisms.

### Manager leverage
Change manager behavior or make desired behavior easier.

### Network effects
Use peers, communities, alumni, experts, or cross-functional networks.

### Feedback loops
Continuously learn from outcomes and update the intervention.

### Governance
Create decision rights and accountability that make execution durable.

A strong solution may use familiar components in an unusually coherent mechanism.

## 4. Anti-generic transformation

Generic:
"Create a mentorship program."

Transform:
"Trigger a 90-day sponsor match when a critical-skill employee has spent 18 months without role-scope expansion; match using target-role skill gaps, give the sponsor two specific access obligations, and measure internal interview conversion."

Generic:
"Train managers."

Transform:
"At quarterly talent review, flag managers with abnormal regrettable attrition after controlling for role and tenure; require a 30-day coaching sprint on the two observed behaviors most associated with exits, followed by skip-level pulse validation."

Generic:
"Use AI for hiring."

Transform:
"Use a structured evidence assistant that maps interview evidence to pre-declared competencies, refuses to infer protected traits, exposes missing evidence before debrief, and logs overrides for bias and quality audits."

The difference is mechanism + context + guardrails + measurement.

## 5. Architecture over initiative lists

Prefer causal architectures such as:

Detect → Decide → Act → Learn

or:

Prevent → Intervene → Recover

or another structure derived from the case.

Each pillar should perform a distinct role in the system.

Avoid lifecycle labels such as:
Attract / Engage / Develop / Retain

unless a true end-to-end redesign is required.

## 6. Human-centered design

For each stakeholder ask:

What are they trying to accomplish?
What friction do they face?
What do they fear losing?
What behavior must change?
Why would they adopt?
What extra work are we creating?
What can be automated?
What requires human judgment?
What happens when the system is wrong?

Never design HR only from HR's perspective.

## 7. Technology and AI

Use technology only where it improves a mechanism.

For AI-enabled solutions define:
- task being augmented,
- data inputs,
- human decision owner,
- explainability need,
- bias/fairness controls,
- privacy,
- prohibited data,
- failure mode,
- override,
- audit trail,
- monitoring.

Do not make "AI-powered" the source of differentiation by itself.

## 9. Adoption

Model adoption as a product funnel:

Eligible
→ Aware
→ Activated
→ First value
→ Repeat use
→ Outcome.

Identify the largest expected drop-off.

Design adoption into the mechanism rather than adding a communication campaign at the end.

## 11. Portfolio discipline

For each proposed component ask:

"If removed, does the architecture become materially weaker?"

If no, remove it.

Case decks gain power from coherence, not volume.
