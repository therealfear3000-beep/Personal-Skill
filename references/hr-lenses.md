# HR Diagnostic Lenses

Use these as optional diagnostic lenses, not as a checklist to force into every case.

## Talent acquisition

Diagnose:
- demand quality,
- sourcing,
- employer proposition,
- screening,
- assessment,
- interviewer calibration,
- candidate experience,
- offer design,
- joining conversion,
- early-tenure quality.

Useful metrics:
- source quality,
- cost per qualified hire,
- time to fill,
- time to productivity,
- offer acceptance,
- quality of hire,
- 6/12-month retention,
- adverse impact.

## Retention

Separate:
- avoidable vs unavoidable,
- regrettable vs non-regrettable,
- early-tenure vs experienced,
- critical-skill vs broad workforce,
- manager-driven vs market-driven.

Potential mechanisms:
- job fit,
- location fit,
- career visibility,
- role scope,
- manager quality,
- pay fairness,
- work design,
- workload,
- team climate,
- identity/belonging,
- mobility friction.

## Performance

Inspect:
- goal clarity,
- measure quality,
- rating calibration,
- manager judgment,
- feedback cadence,
- incentive alignment,
- differentiation,
- development linkage,
- gaming risk.

## Learning and capability

Do not equate learning with courses.

Diagnose:
- capability gap,
- practice opportunity,
- feedback,
- job application,
- manager reinforcement,
- access to real assignments,
- skill visibility,
- credential value.

Prefer learning embedded in work when possible.

## Internal mobility

Inspect:
- opportunity visibility,
- eligibility rules,
- manager release behavior,
- skill matching,
- candidate signaling,
- transition support,
- incentives,
- talent hoarding,
- cross-BU governance.

## Organization design

Inspect:
- layers,
- spans,
- decision rights,
- role clarity,
- interfaces,
- duplicated work,
- coordination load,
- accountability,
- centralization,
- capability ownership.

Do not recommend restructuring unless the causal mechanism requires it.

## Employee experience

Map:
- moments that matter,
- friction,
- effort,
- trust,
- access,
- consistency,
- manager dependency,
- digital/human channel choice.

Focus on behavior and workflow, not only sentiment.

## Wellbeing

Separate:
- individual coping,
- job demands,
- staffing/work design,
- manager behavior,
- access to care,
- trust/privacy,
- crisis support,
- prevention.

Do not respond to structural overload with resilience training alone.

## Rewards

Inspect:
- external competitiveness,
- internal equity,
- pay compression,
- variable-pay logic,
- recognition,
- benefits relevance,
- transparency,
- affordability.

## People analytics / AI

Define:
- decision supported,
- data inputs,
- unit of analysis,
- prediction vs explanation,
- human owner,
- fairness,
- privacy,
- explainability,
- auditability,
- false positive/negative cost,
- model drift,
- employee recourse.

Avoid surveillance-oriented designs.

## Workforce planning

Connect:
business demand
→ workload/capacity
→ role/skill demand
→ supply
→ build/buy/borrow/bot choices
→ location
→ cost
→ risk.

## Change

Diagnose:
- stakeholder incentives,
- capability,
- legitimacy,
- workload,
- local champions,
- governance,
- leadership behavior,
- reinforcement,
- feedback.

"Communication" is rarely the full change strategy.
